Questions

How do I know it's on?

A tool named cue_status appears in your app's tools panel whenever Cue is connected. Its presence is the indicator. Ask your assistant to run it and Cue will confirm it is monitoring.

Why did nothing happen?

Silence is the normal result. Cue speaks only when it finds sycophancy or an unsupported claim — there is no "looks good" message, because a tool that comments on every answer stops being read.

Does Cue see my conversations?

Only the single message being checked, and only for the moment it takes to check it. Nothing you or your AI writes is ever stored. The usage records Cue keeps hold counts and timestamps — never text.

Does it catch everything?

No. Your AI app decides when to call Cue, so coverage depends on the app. Claude is the most consistent today; others call it less often. You can always ask for a check directly.

What does it cost?

Free covers five checks and three rewritten prompts each day, resetting at midnight. Pro is $4.99 a month for unlimited use of both.

What languages does it work in?

Every alert and rewritten prompt comes back in the language you were already writing in — English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Hinglish, Arabic, Spanish, or another. Cue does not switch languages on you.
